## FoodRunner deliveries
FoodRunner pays per delivery: a base pay of ₹25, plus ₹6 per km, plus ₹1 for each minute you wait at the restaurant beyond the first 5 minutes. During lunch (12-2 PM) and dinner (7-10 PM) peaks, a boost of 1.2x to 1.5x is applied. FoodRunner does not take a commission from delivery partners. Daily streak bonuses: ₹100 when you complete 10 deliveries in a day, and another ₹150 at 15 deliveries.

## RideNow bike taxi rides
RideNow fares are ₹20 base, plus ₹9 per km, plus ₹1 per minute of ride time, multiplied by surge when demand is high. RideNow keeps an 18% commission on the fare. Customer tips are never commissioned. Daily streak bonus: ₹150 at 12 rides.

## QuickDrop grocery deliveries
QuickDrop pays ₹30 base plus ₹7 per km per delivery, with peak boosts. No commission. Daily streak bonuses: ₹80 at 8 deliveries and another ₹160 at 14 deliveries.

## Why one trip pays less than another
The biggest factors are distance (short trips pay mostly the base amount), whether the trip happened in a peak window, waiting time, and, on RideNow, commission. Streak bonuses are added to the trip that reaches the target, so that trip looks unusually large and the others look smaller. Tips go 100% to you. Every trip in GigPay shows a line-by-line breakdown.

## Earning more per hour
Your earnings per hour depend on trips per hour and pay per trip. Working the lunch and dinner peaks, staying close to busy restaurant clusters, finishing daily streaks, and avoiding long empty returns usually help the most.
