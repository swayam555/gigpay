## Life cover: PMJJBY
Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana gives ₹2 lakh of life cover for death from any cause, for an annual premium of ₹436, auto-debited from your bank account. Ages 18 to 50 can join. The cover year runs from 1 June to 31 May.

## Accident cover: PMSBY
Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana gives ₹2 lakh for accidental death or total disability and ₹1 lakh for partial disability, for about ₹20 a year. Ages 18 to 70 can join. For anyone who rides all day, this is one of the most valuable ₹20 you can spend.

## Pension: Atal Pension Yojana
Atal Pension Yojana gives a guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 from age 60, depending on how much you contribute. You can join between ages 18 and 40 through your bank. The earlier you join, the smaller the monthly contribution.

## An emergency fund
Try to keep at least one month of expenses in a separate savings account. Even ₹50 a day adds up to about ₹1,500 a month. With an emergency fund you won't need a high-interest loan when the bike breaks down.
