title: Earned Wage Access vs Instant Loan Apps
source: GigPay guidance, with reference to RBI's guidance on digital lending and the RBI Sachet portal for complaints.
---

## The difference
Earned wage access, such as a GigPay instant payout, gives you your own money early for a small flat fee. A loan is someone else's money that you must repay with interest. Instant loan apps can charge very high effective interest and fees, and some unregistered apps harass borrowers.

## Warning signs of an illegal loan app
- It is not linked to a bank or an RBI-registered NBFC, and doesn't name its lending partner.
- It asks for access to your contacts and photos.
- The amount you receive is much smaller than the loan amount, because of hidden deductions.
- It threatens you or contacts your family or friends.

## What to do
Borrow only from banks or RBI-regulated lenders, and read the key fact statement, which shows the total cost of the loan. Report unauthorised lending apps to the RBI's Sachet portal (sachet.rbi.org.in) and online harassment to the cyber crime helpline 1930. If you just need cash before payday, an instant payout of money you've already earned is far cheaper than any loan.
