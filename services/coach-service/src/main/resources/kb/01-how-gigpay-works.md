## Getting paid for work you've already done
GigPay gives you access to money you have already earned on FoodRunner, RideNow and QuickDrop, before the platform's weekly payout. Every completed trip is added to your GigPay balance within seconds. This is called earned wage access. It is not a loan: there is no interest, no credit check and nothing to repay, because the money is yours.

## Instant payouts and their limits
- You can withdraw up to 90% of your balance instantly to any UPI ID. The remaining 10% protects against platform adjustments, such as cancelled orders or penalties applied after the trip.
- Each instant payout has a flat fee of ₹5. The minimum is ₹100.
- You can make up to 3 instant payouts a day, up to ₹5,000 in total per day.
- Money usually reaches your bank account within seconds.

## The free weekly payout
Whatever balance remains is paid out automatically and for free in the weekly payout, to the UPI ID registered on your profile. If you never use instant payouts, you pay nothing.

## When a payout fails
Banks are sometimes down, or a UPI ID is mistyped. If a payout cannot be completed, GigPay retries automatically for temporary bank errors. If it still fails, the full amount and the fee are returned to your GigPay balance, usually within a minute. You can then try again with the correct UPI ID. You never lose money because of a failed payout.

## Your statement
Every rupee that enters or leaves your GigPay balance appears in your statement, with the trip or payout it relates to. GigPay uses double-entry accounting, the same method banks use, so the balance always equals the sum of your statement lines.
