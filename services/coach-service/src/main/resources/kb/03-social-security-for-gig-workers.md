---
title: Social Security for Gig and Platform Workers in India
source: Summary of the Code on Social Security 2020, Union Budget 2025-26 announcements and Labour Ministry advisories. Check official portals for the latest status.
---

## You are recognised in law
The Code on Social Security, 2020 recognised "gig workers" and "platform workers" for the first time and provides for social security schemes for them, including life and disability cover, health and maternity benefits and old-age protection, to be notified by the central and state governments.

## Register on e-Shram
e-Shram is the national database of unorganised workers run by the Ministry of Labour and Employment. The Union Budget 2025-26 announced that online platform workers would be registered on e-Shram, given identity cards, and covered for healthcare under Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY). In March 2025 the Labour Ministry asked platform workers to self-register on the e-Shram portal (eshram.gov.in) so they could access these benefits. You need your Aadhaar-linked mobile number and bank account details.

## Ayushman Bharat health cover
AB-PMJAY provides health cover of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for hospitalisation at empanelled public and private hospitals. Check your eligibility and get your Ayushman card through the official portal or a nearby common service centre.

## How big is the gig workforce
NITI Aayog estimated 77 lakh gig workers in India in 2020-21 and projected 2.35 crore by 2029-30.
