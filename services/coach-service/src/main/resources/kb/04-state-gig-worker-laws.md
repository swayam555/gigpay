title: State Laws for Platform-Based Gig Workers
source: Summary of the Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers (Registration and Welfare) Act 2023 and the Karnataka Platform-Based Gig Workers (Social Security and Welfare) Ordinance and Bill 2025. Implementation depends on rules notified by each state.
---

## Rajasthan
Rajasthan was the first state to pass a law for platform-based gig workers, in 2023. It provides for registration of gig workers and aggregators, a welfare board, and a welfare fund financed through a fee on aggregators. Enforcement of key provisions depends on the state notifying its rules.

## Karnataka
Karnataka promulgated an ordinance for platform-based gig workers in May 2025 and introduced a bill to replace it in August 2025. It provides for a welfare fee of 1% to 5% of the payout to gig workers, levied on aggregators such as food delivery, ride-hailing and e-commerce platforms (the exact rate to be notified), paid into a social security and welfare fund. It also provides for registration of workers, grievance redressal, and transparency about how automated systems affect workers' pay and work.

## What to do
If you work in one of these states, watch for registration drives by the state labour department or welfare board, and register when they open. Registration is usually needed to receive welfare benefits.
