## Scams that target delivery and ride partners
- A "customer" sends a UPI collect request or a QR code and says it is a refund or an extra tip. Entering your UPI PIN always sends money out; you never need a PIN to receive money.
- A caller pretending to be from the platform or GigPay support asks for an OTP, your PIN or screen sharing to "fix your payout". Real support never asks for these.
- Fake job or "task" offers ask you to receive money into your account and send it on. This makes your account a money mule, which is illegal and can get your account frozen.

## If something goes wrong
Call the national cyber crime helpline 1930 immediately or report on cybercrime.gov.in, then tell your bank. Acting within the first few hours gives the best chance of stopping the money. Change your UPI PIN, and contact GigPay support from inside the app.
